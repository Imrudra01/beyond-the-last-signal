## 🌑 About the Project

What happens when a machine designed to survive 90 days explores another planet for nearly 15 years?

**Beyond the Last Signal** is an interactive digital storytelling experience that brings the extraordinary journey of NASA's Opportunity rover to life.

Rather than presenting mission history through conventional timelines and technical reports, we combine cinematic storytelling, authentic NASA imagery, and interactive elements to make planetary exploration engaging, accessible, and memorable.

Our goal is simple: **make people care about a mission they may never have known existed.**

## 🪐 The Experience

The website takes visitors through seven chapters of Opportunity's journey:

- 🚀 **The Big Trip to Mars:** Launch, interplanetary journey, and landing.
- ⏳ **A 90-Day Promise:** The beginning of an unexpectedly long mission.
- 🔬 **The Clue in the Rocks:** Evidence of ancient water on Mars.
- 🏜️ **Trapped on Another World:** The Purgatory Dune incident.
- 🌪️ **The Rover That Kept Going:** Challenges, discoveries, and extraordinary endurance.
- 📡 **The Last Signal:** The 2018 dust storm and final communication.
- ❤️ **Abandoned, But Not Forgotten:** The mission's conclusion and lasting legacy.

### 🔓 The Final Memories

The storytelling experience continues beyond the final chapter.

Visitors must complete a **seven-question interactive quiz**, combining technical and ethical questions, to unlock two final visual experiences:

1. 📸 **Opportunity's Final 360° Panorama:** A view of Perseverance Valley captured by the rover itself in 2018.
2. 🛰️ **Opportunity's Final Resting Place:** An orbital photograph showing the location where the rover came to rest.

These experiences turn the conclusion into an opportunity for discovery and reflection.

## ✨ What Makes It Different?

- **Story-first approach:** Scientific history presented as an immersive narrative.
- **Authentic imagery:** Real NASA mission photographs rather than AI-generated representations.
- **Interactive learning:** A knowledge challenge that tests understanding and encourages reflection.
- **Progressive discovery:** Important final mission imagery is unlocked through participation.
- **Emotional storytelling:** Connects scientific achievements with the human experience of exploration.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

Frontend: Vanilla HTML5, Three.js (3D WebGL Rover Visualization), Web Audio API, Lucide Icons

Styling: Modern CSS3 (Glassmorphism, CSS Grid/Flexbox, Custom Properties, Responsive Tactical HUD)

Programming: JavaScript (ES6+), HTML5, CSS3, WebGL

Mission imagery: NASA / JPL-Caltech archives & Planetary Data System (PDS)

Deployment: Netlify
